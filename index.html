Capacity steps (threshold targets)
Each step target = [ WorkDays×(hrs/shift + OT) + Holidays×(hrs/shift + Holiday OT) ] × shifts. Adjust the values to move where each threshold line sits.
Day capacity lines
Day view only. Each line = (hrs/shift + OT + break-relief) × shifts, or a fixed hours/day. No Max Cap line in Day view.
Week capacity lines
Week view only — the monthly WorkDays/OT/holiday formula combined with break-relief & a fixed hours/day override. [ WD×(hrs/shift + break-relief) + OT_days×OT + Holidays×… ] × shifts, or Fixed h/day × WD. WorkDays ≈ 5–7. Week keeps the Max Cap line.
